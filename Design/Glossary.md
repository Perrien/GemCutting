# GemCutting Glossary

A Mac/iPad app where players facet gemstones by following the real cutting process; payout is
scored against a design's complexity and the precision of the cut.

## Language

**Pattern**:
The published recipe for one faceted cut — the tiers it is built from, and for each tier its angle,
its index positions and the meet it is cut to. A pattern describes proportions rather than a size, so
it is not a stone until it is cut to one.
_Avoid_: design (overloaded — also the documents folder and the project's own design records), cut
(also the verb for the act of faceting)

**Cheater**:
A small adjustment to a facet's angle or index, beyond the machine's fixed index-wheel stops, made
to correct a meet-point error so every facet lines up and the surface polishes out cleanly.
_Avoid_: cheat, index cheat

**Preform**:
A rough stone shaped into an intermediate blank before faceting begins — not itself a finished cut
design.
_Avoid_: blank (ambiguous with rough material in general)

**Mast height**:
The vertical position of the machine's mast/quill relative to the lap, set independently of a
facet's angle. It determines the depth at which a given angle is reached, so getting it right is
what makes the target angle and the design's meet point (see meet forms in
`design-authoring-format.md`) arrive together.
_Avoid_: mast angle (a different setting — the facet's angle itself, not the mast's height)

**Mast angle**:
The angle a facet is cut at, measured from the table plane — the table sits at 0°, the girdle at
90°. Set independently of mast height (see Mast height).
_Avoid_: cutting angle
