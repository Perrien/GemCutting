# The Awaiting-Facets Prompt Does Not Name Them

Status: untriaged
Filed: 2026-08-27

When a pick is waiting for the third facet through a corner, the status strip reads *click one of 2
facets through the point* without saying which two, so the only way to find them is to spot the
candidate chips in the viewport — and those sit at facet centroids, which can be far from the clicked
corner or off-screen entirely. On `Novice Ash-er`'s `P2` the two offered facets are the girdle facets
above the corner, whose centres are near or past the top edge of the frame at the default camera.
